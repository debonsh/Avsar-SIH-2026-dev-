About this project · founder vision + interpretation

Prove skills, not claims

One roof for careers: find openings, close skill gaps for free, prove what you learned, and show it to the world. Jobs, people, clans, coaching and verified proof in a single loop.

01Your thoughts, kept intact

Founder notes

LinkedIn-like connecting plus Naukri-like listings plus AI help plus freebies plus upskilling: resume review that finds gaps and shows how to raise the ATS score. Gamified with leaderboard (still under discussion), XP, clans, and a profile that shows certificates, GitHub and LeetCode as badges worth grinding for, with a dopamine loop that keeps people improving. Auto roadmaps with todo lists. Mock interviews, mock tests, proof of work, and post-certification skill tests that award showcaseable badges. Portfolio styled your way. Clan and people chat in a simple Discord-like way: mutual clans and badges visible, no follower counts. Companies hire full dev teams fast instead of hunting individuals. Many apps under one roof, made easy.

02My interpretation: the wedge

The roof is phase three. The wedge that wins the hackathon is one closed loop for one user with zero other users: diagnose, fix free, prove, unlock, showcase. Everything social (clans, chat, seasons) and everything marketplace (squad-hire) hangs off verified proof, so proof ships first. The trust currency is MAIN plus badges plus evidence links. Nothing else needs to exist for the demo to be real.

05Research fuel (cited, usable in viva)

85%employers use skills-based hiring (TestGorilla 2025)
76%use skills tests; resumes falling (73 to 67%)
+25%lesson completion from leagues (Duolingo)
12→55%next-day retention via streaks (Duolingo)
+22%daily completion with friend streaks
+7%entry-level hiring growth vs +19% senior (HackerRank 2025): the pain is real

Field evidence backs the mechanics: AI-generated questions test comparably to expert ones in a 91-class study when a generate-judge-refine loop plus distractor QA is used, which is exactly how cert tests should be built (cache the approved bank, never generate live on stage). Leagues work because pools are small (20 to 30), evenly matched, and reset weekly with tightening promotion bands, which is the template for clan seasons. Streaks need forgiveness (freezes) and milestone payouts with real value, not confetti.
