# Avsar reference board — viewed, not inferred

## Design read
Persuade surface (landing) in front of an Operate app. Audience: Tier-2/3 Indian
college students + placement cells. Lane: dark developer-tool SaaS with a learning
platform's warmth. Dials: low variance, quiet motion, dense proof, art-directed
product mockups (no stock).

## Direct competitors
- Handshake / Internshala: job boards with no skill-gap loop. Take: their job-card
  density. Reject: light corporate chrome, star-rating noise.

## Adjacent products (viewed 2026-09-16)
- Linear (linear.app): centered hero, one H1, product UI full-bleed below in a
  window frame. Alternating rows: sticky text label left, product visual right,
  feature chips under. Numbered figures (Fig 0.1), changelog, quote wall, stat
  band, sitemap footer. Take: the whole page skeleton.
- Vercel (vercel.com): three-up proof cells pairing each feature with a customer
  outcome. "Recently shipped" cards. Take: proof-per-cell, changelog energy.
- Duolingo (known): streaks, XP, leagues, skill tree, celebration motion. Take:
  progress-as-reward on the Quests page only. Reject: cartoon mascot voice.

## Interface systems
- shadcn: cva variants, Slot, muted/border tokens. Already in ui.jsx.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI landing: split hero, 3 icon cards, metric strip, gradient CTA.
- Cream + serif + terracotta editorial look. Neon-glow dark gamer look.

## Direction contract
Thesis: the product UI is the hero. First viewport: centered Bricolage headline,
two CTAs, then a full-width window-framed score mockup. Risk: mockups must stay
honest (label illustrative values).
