C2C · Campus2Corporate · explainer

One roof for getting hired

Find openings, close your skill gaps, prove what you learned, and show it to the world. Jobs, people, clans, coaching and verified proof in a single loop, instead of five separate apps.

vite + react 19 + tailwind v4 · no backend in build 1, browser storage only · build passes · 48/48 tests green

Explain it in 30 seconds

"Applying everywhere and hearing nothing is the problem I lived. C2C scores your resume transparently, tells you exactly what to learn, tests whether you learned it, and proves it with badges employers can trust. Today it works solo. Next come clans. Later, companies hire whole verified squads." Memorize this order: problem, loop, proof, roadmap.

01Problem

Students apply everywhere and hear back nowhere. They cannot see why they were rejected, what to learn next, or how to prove new skills. Recruiters drown in unverified resumes. Clans, chats, job boards and course sites each solve one slice and none of them connect.

02The loop

  1. 01AnalyzeResume scored 0 to 95, gaps listed
  2. 02UpskillQuiz, quests, free courses, roadmap
  3. 03ProveTests, interviews, evidence links
  4. 04MatchJobs unlock as the score rises
  5. 05ShowcasePortfolio, badges, kudos, battle

Finishing the loop re-scores you, which re-opens the loop. That cycle is the whole retention design: every action moves a number you can see.

03How scoring works

ATS · resume0 to 95
+
Quiz · best grade0 to 100
+
Proof · quest pairs × 200 to 100
=
MAIN · weighted0 to 100
tech tracks (sde, data):      MAIN = round(0.5*ATS + 0.3*Quiz + 0.2*Proof)
other tracks (mktg, govt):    MAIN = round(0.6*ATS + 0.3*Quiz + 0.1*Proof)

Example: ATS 80, quiz 100, 5 quest pairs (proof 100). Tech: 40 + 30 + 20 = 90, Diamond. Non-tech: 48 + 30 + 10 = 88, Platinum. Ranks: Diamond 90+, Platinum 80+, Gold 65+, Silver 50+, Bronze below. Each job sets a minimum MAIN, so the rank is the lock and the key. Logic lives in app/src/lib/score.js and app/src/lib/scores.js, pinned by app/tests/scores.test.js.

06Order of attack

Phase 1, single player: score, roadmap, badge, portfolio, jobs. Must stand alone with zero other users. Phase 2, social: clans, chat, seasons, behind moderation and density. Phase 3, marketplace: squad-hire for companies. Each phase funds the next one's cold start. Anything not on this list is cut, not postponed.

07Glossary

ATS, resume score 0 to 95. MAIN, combined readiness 0 to 100. Proof, verified quest work 0 to 100. Quest pair, course plus project plus evidence link. XP, points for verified actions only. Clan, skill group with shared chat. Badge, scarce test-earned proof.
